How I approach procurement scenarios

Illustrative workflows based on responsibilities, not confidential employer case studies

When delivery is delayed

Verify the supplier's revised schedule, assess operational priority, identify technically acceptable alternatives, coordinate approvals, and document the escalation and revised plan.

Before bulk printing

Request a physical sample or proof, compare it with approved artwork and specifications, obtain the relevant department's confirmation, then authorize the next stage within the purchasing process.

When comparing offers

Check specification compliance first, then compare the full commercial terms: price, quality, lead time, logistics, contractual documents and the supplier's delivery reliability.
